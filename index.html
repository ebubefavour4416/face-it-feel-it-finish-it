The problem

If this sounds like you, you're not broken. You're stuck.

You keep replaying it

The same memories, the same conversations, on a loop you can't seem to turn off, even months later.

You blame yourself

A quiet voice keeps telling you it was your fault, or that you're not enough, and it's exhausting to argue with.

"Time will heal it" hasn't worked

You've waited. You've tried distracting yourself. But the feeling hasn't actually moved, it's just gone quiet.

A familiar story

"I told myself I was fine. I wasn't."

She kept busy. New gym routine, new friends, a packed calendar. From the outside, she looked completely over it. But every time his name came up, or a song played, or she passed the restaurant they used to go to, the same tight feeling returned in her chest.

She wasn't avoiding the breakup on purpose. She just didn't know there was anything else to do besides wait it out. Nobody had ever explained that healing is a process with real steps, not just a countdown clock.

Why the usual advice doesn't work

"Just move on" was never a real instruction.

Generic tips skip the actual process

"Delete their number" or "stay busy" might distract you for a while, but they don't address why you're stuck in the first place.

Time alone doesn't resolve unprocessed feelings

Research on emotional recovery shows that avoided feelings don't fade with time, they stay frozen until they're actually worked through.

You don't know what's actually hurting

Most people feel a big, tangled ball of sadness and anger without being able to name what's underneath it, which makes it impossible to resolve.

The solution

The Face It, Feel It, Finish It Framework

A 3-step process, grounded in real psychological research on emotional recovery, that walks you from feeling frozen to genuinely at peace.

STEP 1

Face It

Stop avoiding the reminders and the feelings. Learn to tolerate what comes up instead of numbing it, so real change can begin.

STEP 2

Feel It

Separate the tangled sadness and anger. Find the deeper hurt underneath, and understand what you truly need to feel whole again.

STEP 3

Finish It

Move through healthy anger and real grief to genuine closure, whether that means forgiveness, distance, or simply letting go.

What changes for you

By the end, you'll finally feel like yourself again.

Process the real hurt

Move past the surface anger or numbness to what's actually underneath, where real healing starts.

Reach genuine closure

Get to a real ending, on your terms, instead of staying in limbo indefinitely.

A process, not just a countdown

Stop waiting for time to fix it, and start actively working through it, at your own pace.
